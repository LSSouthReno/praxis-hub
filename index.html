Mission & values

A seminary for pastors

Praxis focuses on training pastors, aspiring and current, because the local church is God’s plan A to reach a lost world. It is a hybrid seminary on a trimester system: most reading and coursework happens online, and each trimester gathers for a three-day intensive in Reno. Students stay rooted in their local church, keep their jobs, and finish a four-year degree formed by doctrine, application, formation and inspiration.

Doctrine

What every professor affirms

Historic Christianity

  • Apostles’, Nicene and Athanasian Creeds
  • Chicago Statement on Inerrancy
  • Lausanne Covenant
  • Gospel Coalition Confessional Statement

Praxis distinctives

  • Historically Reformed · the five Solas; God sovereign in all of salvation
  • Complementarian · qualified men fill the office of elder
  • Missional · sent by the Great Commission to seek the lost
  • Spirit-dependent · the Spirit is essential to effective ministry
  • Christ-centered · in hermeneutic and proclamation

Kingdom synergy with the 1689 Baptist, Westminster, Belgic and Second Helvetic confessions, and with networks such as Living Stones, Acts 29, SEND, Harbor, One Gospel, PCA, OPC and 1689 Baptists.

Degrees & tracks

Two four-year degrees

MABS · Master of Biblical Studies

For preaching pastors, lead pastors and church planters. Core: Law, Prophets, Writings, Gospels/Acts, Epistles–Revelation.

MATS · Master of Applied Theological Studies

For executive and associate pastors. Core: God’s Image, God’s Attributes, God’s Gospel, OT Survey, NT Survey.

Courses

Shared doctrinal core and twelve practitioner courses

Both degrees share Biblical Church Leadership, Hermeneutics, Church History, Ethics, Systematic Theology, Biblical Theology and Ecclesiology. Doctrinal courses are taught by scholars with a PhD or DMin; practitioner courses by tenured pastors with a master’s or higher.

Faculty expectations

Praxis pays for quality and expects excellence

Teaching

  • Syllabus and requirements delivered before the trimester begins
  • All nine lectures per intensive taught in person
  • Student messages answered within 24–48 business hours
  • Grades and evaluations submitted within 30 days of the due date
  • Expectations set with the proctor before and during the term

Life and conduct

  • Order of life: God, wife, children, then ministry
  • Active, faithful membership in a local church
  • Casual but respectable dress; West Coast standards in public
  • A culture of honor toward colleagues and students
  • Public and social media speech that is convictional, winsome and charitable
  • Christian liberty exercised in love; drunkenness forbidden

FAQ

Questions we get asked

  • Accredited? Not at launch. Accreditation will be pursued only if it serves the mission.
  • Who can enroll? Members in good standing commended by their pastor, aspiring or active pastors, serving in a local church. Praxis trains men for the office of pastor.
  • Scholarships? Potentially for stage-two church planters. The tuition itself functions like a 50% scholarship against other seminaries.
  • Faster track? No. Four years is deliberate; formation takes time. One class per trimester is allowed and doubles the timeline.
  • Doctoral programs? No. Praxis trains pastors, not researchers.
  • Denomination? None. Praxis has kingdom synergy with several reformed networks and denominations.
